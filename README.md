# AI-Prompts

*A collection of AI prompts created, tested, and refined through human–AI collaboration.*

Welcome to **AI-Prompts**, a growing collection of prompts I've developed through experimenting and collaborating with artificial intelligence.

I'm not a computer scientist or an AI expert. I don't have a formal background in either field. I'm simply someone who's curious about what AI can do and enjoys exploring ways to make it more useful.

Rather than approaching AI as an expert, I approach it as a collaborator. I use AI to brainstorm, develop, structure, and refine my prompts, learning through experimentation along the way.

I'm sharing this collection in the hope that others will find something useful, build upon these ideas, and perhaps teach me a thing or two in return.

## What's in This Repository?

This repository contains prompts covering areas such as:

- **Learning & Education** — Prompts for exploring topics, understanding difficult concepts, and improving how we learn.
- **Creative Work** — Prompts that support brainstorming, ideation, and creative exploration.
- **Writing** — Prompts for drafting, editing, organizing thoughts, and improving written communication.
- **Productivity** — Prompts designed to help organize tasks, approach problems, and streamline workflows.
- **AI Agents** — Prompts for experimenting with more structured, multi-step AI behaviors and workflows.

The collection will grow and evolve as I continue experimenting.

## How to Use These Prompts

You're welcome to explore the collection and try anything that interests you.

1. **Browse** the repository and find a prompt that suits your needs.
2. **Copy** the prompt into your preferred AI assistant.
3. **Customize** it to fit your situation, goals, or preferences.
4. **Experiment** with different approaches and see what works best for you.

These prompts are intended as starting points, not perfect or universal solutions.

AI systems can interpret the same instructions differently, so results may vary depending on the model, context, and how you use the prompt.

## A Note on How These Prompts Are Made

I want to be transparent about my process.

**These prompts are created with the assistance of AI.**

I often start with an idea or a problem I'd like to solve. From there, I work with AI to explore possible approaches, write instructions, revise their structure, and improve the results.

I don't have formal training in computer science, prompt engineering, or artificial intelligence. My approach is driven by curiosity, practical experimentation, and a willingness to learn.

I don't claim that these prompts are technically perfect, scientifically validated, or necessarily the most efficient solutions available.

They're works in progress, and I expect my understanding and methods to improve over time.

## Feedback & Contributions

**Feedback is very welcome, especially constructive criticism.**

One of the reasons I'm making this repository public is to learn from people who may have more experience or different perspectives.

If you notice:

- Mistakes, unclear instructions, or incorrect assumptions
- Unnecessary complexity or inefficient prompt structures
- Potential limitations, biases, or unexpected behaviors
- Better ways to achieve the same results
- Opportunities to make a prompt more accessible or useful

Please feel free to share your thoughts.

### How to Contribute

You can:

- **Open an Issue** to report a problem, suggest an improvement, or start a discussion.
- **Submit a Pull Request** with a proposed revision or improvement to an existing prompt.
- **Share your experience** using a prompt, including what worked and what didn't.

Contributions will be reviewed before being incorporated into the collection.

I particularly appreciate feedback that explains *why* a change might improve a prompt. Understanding the reasoning helps me learn, rather than simply replacing one version with another.

Respectful disagreement and thoughtful criticism are encouraged.
