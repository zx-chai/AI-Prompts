# AI-Prompts

*A collection of AI prompts developed and refined through human–AI collaboration.*

Welcome to **AI-Prompts**, a growing collection of prompts I've developed through experimentation and collaboration with artificial intelligence.

I'm not a computer scientist or an AI expert. I don't have a formal background in either field. I'm simply curious about what AI can do and interested in finding ways to make it more useful.

I approach AI as a collaborator, using it to brainstorm, develop, structure, and refine prompts while learning through experimentation.

I'm sharing this collection in the hope that others will find something useful, build upon these ideas, and perhaps teach me something along the way.

## What's in This Repository?

This repository contains prompts covering areas such as:

- **Learning & Education** — Exploring topics, understanding difficult concepts, and improving learning.
- **Creative Work** — Brainstorming, generating ideas, and exploring creative possibilities.
- **Writing** — Drafting, editing, organizing thoughts, and improving communication.
- **Productivity** — Organizing tasks, solving problems, and improving workflows.
- **AI Agents** — Experimenting with structured, multi-step AI behaviors and workflows.

The collection will grow and evolve as I continue experimenting.

## How to Use These Prompts

You're welcome to explore the collection and try anything that interests you.

1. **Browse** the repository and find a prompt that suits your needs.
2. **Copy** the prompt into your preferred AI assistant.
3. **Customize** it to fit your goals, situation, or preferences.
4. **Experiment** with different approaches and see what works best.

These prompts are starting points, not perfect or universal solutions.

AI systems may interpret identical instructions differently. Results can vary depending on the model, context, and how the prompt is used.

## How These Prompts Are Made

I want to be transparent about my process.

**These prompts are developed with the assistance of AI.**

I typically begin with an idea, question, or problem I'd like to solve. From there, I collaborate with AI to explore possible approaches, draft instructions, refine their structure, and evaluate potential improvements.

I don't have formal training in computer science, prompt engineering, or artificial intelligence.

My approach is driven by curiosity, practical experimentation, and a willingness to learn.

I don't claim that these prompts are technically perfect, scientifically validated, or necessarily the most efficient solutions available.

They're works in progress, and I expect both my understanding and my methods to improve over time.

## Feedback & Contributions

**Feedback is welcome, especially constructive criticism.**

One of the main reasons I'm making this repository public is to learn from people with different experiences, expertise, and perspectives.

If you notice:

- Mistakes, unclear instructions, or incorrect assumptions
- Unnecessary complexity or inefficient prompt structures
- Potential limitations, biases, or unexpected behaviors
- Better ways to achieve the same results
- Opportunities to make a prompt more accessible or useful

Please feel free to share your thoughts.

### How to Contribute

You can:

- **Open an Issue** to report a problem, suggest an improvement, or start a discussion.
- **Submit a Pull Request** proposing changes to an existing prompt.
- **Share your experiences** using a prompt, including what worked and what didn't.

Contributions will be reviewed before being incorporated into the collection.

I particularly appreciate feedback that explains *why* a change might improve a prompt. Understanding the reasoning helps me learn rather than simply replacing one version with another.

Respectful disagreement and thoughtful criticism are encouraged.
